# User Manual - SmartWalletAI

### 1. Introduction
SmartWalletAI is your personal financial assistant. This guide helps you navigate the features of the app to manage your budget effectively.

### 2. Getting Started
*   **Registration:** Sign up using your email and a secure password.
*   **Profile Setup:** On your first login, complete your profile by adding your name and a profile picture.
*   **Biometric Security:** Go to settings to enable Fingerprint/Face unlock for added security.

### 3. Core Functionality
*   **Dashboard:** View your total balance and recent transactions.
*   **Adding Expenses:** 
    *   Tap the **Floating Action Button (+)**.
    *   Manual Entry: Type in the category, amount, and date.
    *   **AI Scan:** Click the camera icon to scan a physical receipt. The app will automatically extract the amount and date.
*   **Analytics:** Visit the 'Transactions' or 'Stats' tab to see a visual breakdown of your spending via interactive charts.
*   **Budget Alerts:** Set a monthly budget and receive notifications when you are close to the limit.

### 4. Frequently Asked Questions
*   **How do I reset my password?** Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen.
*   **Is my data safe?** Yes, all data is encrypted via Firebase and protected by your device's biometrics.
