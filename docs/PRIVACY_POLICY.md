# Privacy Policy for SmartWalletAI

**Last Updated:** May 2024

**SmartWalletAI** ("the App") is developed as a university project and is committed to protecting user privacy.

### 1. Information Collection
We collect minimal data required for the app to function:
*   **Authentication Data:** Email and password managed securely by Firebase Auth.
*   **Financial Data:** Transaction records stored in Firebase Firestore.
*   **Profile Images:** Stored in Firebase Storage.

### 2. Biometric Data
The app utilizes the **Android Biometric API**. 
*   **Important:** The App **does not access, store, or transmit** your fingerprint or facial recognition data. Authentication is handled entirely by the Android Operating System's secure enclave.

### 3. Permissions Usage
*   **Camera:** To scan receipts for AI parsing.
*   **Notifications:** To send daily reminders and budget alerts.
*   **Storage:** To select and upload profile pictures.

### 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share your financial data with third-party companies. All data is used strictly within the context of the user's account.
